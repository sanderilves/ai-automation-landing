Co-funded by the European Union and Enterprise Estonia (EIS)

Project Description

Automating repetitive manual tasks using software, so that employees can focus on higher value-added work. We develop business process automation solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises using artificial intelligence and modern automation platforms. The solutions replace repetitive manual workflows with software, without the company needing to hire an IT specialist or purchase expensive software licences.

Project Goal

To deliver automation solutions that free SME employees from manual, repetitive tasks — enabling them to focus on work that requires human judgment, expertise, and creativity.
